This is the working mock-up behind the Assessment 2 plan. Walk Chloe's path from the question she asks in a TikTok thread to the price she can see without giving anyone her email. Every route lands on the same price page, and the address bar shows how each route is tagged.

What to notice. The price appears before any personal detail is asked for. Each route carries its own tag (utm_source=tiktok, utm_source=clinic&clinic=IW01, and so on), which is how the plan attributes movement to the page. The billed total is shown at least as prominently as the per-day figure. Nothing you type is stored.
