Tactic 4: the consult-room card

Where a vet judges fresh food suitable, the vet hands over this card. Scanning it opens the price page with a clinic code, so the plan can see which clinics the visits came from.

lyka

What fresh food costs for your dog

If your vet has suggested fresh food, this shows the ongoing price before you sign up for anything.

$15.50 a day
Example: 30 kg adult Labrador, Full Bowl, $217.00 every 2 weeks, list price before offers, 17 Sep 2026

Back of card: "Scan to see your dog's price. No email needed." plus the disclosure that the card is produced by Lyka and supplied to participating clinics under a flat arrangement with no payment per sale.

Measures and limits

Clinics participating, cards handed over and QR-sourced sessions by clinic code. A scan shows movement to the page, not confidence or purchase. Pilot of up to ten clinics in Sydney's inner west; clinic willingness is an assumption.
