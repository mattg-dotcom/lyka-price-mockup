Sample reply script (20 to 30 seconds)

"You asked how much. As an example, a 30 kg adult Labrador on Full Bowl was $217 every two weeks at list price in September 2026, before introductory offers. Half Bowl, which covers half the diet, was $257.60 every four weeks. Your own dog's price is on the page linked in our bio, and you don't need to give us an email to see it."

How it is measured

Supporting measure: at least 90 per cent of eligible public price questions in the final four weeks answered publicly within seven days with a price and the address, reported as counts by platform. Taps on the profile (bio) link are tagged utm_source=tiktok.

Responsible practice

Captions on every video; commenter handles blurred unless they agree; a price answer never makes a health claim; evidence questions answered with the source; a health complaint acknowledged publicly, then handled privately by the vet team.
