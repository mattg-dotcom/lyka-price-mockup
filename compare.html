Is there an introductory discount?Yes, on the first deliveries. This page shows the ongoing price, which is what you will pay after that.
Do I have to give my email to see my dog's price?No. The price page shows your dog's estimate first. Email is optional, after the answer.
Why is Half Bowl more per kilo?Smaller portions cost more to pack and ship per kilo. The page shows both so you can compare.
Can I pause or cancel?Yes, from your account, at any time.
